# [GBatNet Big Bat Database Working Group](https://www.gbatnet.org/interdisciplinary-projects/#big-bat-database)
We were established as the BigBatDatabase Working Group, part of the Global Union of Bat Diversity Networks (GBatNet), the network of bat networks and affiliated organizations working on complementary aspects of bat biology to facilitate and enhance bat research and conservation.
