## Join BigBatDatabase

BigBatDatabase is a community-driven initiative to compile, standardize, and share bat trait data for research, education, and conservation. We welcome researchers, students, collection managers, and other contributors interested in bat biodiversity and data synthesis.

If you have any questions or would like to join our Slack community, please contact us at [bigbatdatabase@gmail.com](mailto:bigbatdatabase@gmail.com).

To help us process your request, please briefly introduce yourself and provide a link to a publication, professional profile, or another online resource that allows us to verify your identity.

## Contribute without joining Slack

If you prefer not to use Slack but would still like to contribute and be findable by other members of the community, please complete [this form](YOUR_FORM_LINK). Please share your name, email address, and areas of expertise by geographic region, taxonomic group, and discipline.
